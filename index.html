Latest version

v1.8.0 (build 16)
• Importing your own question file works again. Files that appeared greyed out and could not be tapped — anything downloaded in Safari, sent by AirDrop, or kept in a cloud folder — can now be picked. Large files no longer make the app sit still while they load. And when a file is in iCloud but has not been downloaded to the phone yet, the app now says so, instead of doing nothing at all when you tap it. • Question banks can be sent out about ten times smaller. A published bank can now be compressed, so there is roughly a tenth as much to download and about a tenth as much work to open it. The difference is biggest on older phones, where opening a full bank could take several seconds on its own. Nothing changes for banks already on your phone. • Android no longer takes the app's security key into its own backup. Android was quietly backing up and restoring the app's stored security data, and a restored copy could no longer be unlocked on the phone it landed on. That is what left some phones asking you to sign in every single time you opened the app. The app now keeps itself out of Android's backup entirely. Your progress is still backed up to Google Drive by the app itself, exactly as before. • A phone with a broken security store now says so. If the app cannot remember your sign-in two launches running, the sign-in screen explains why and tells you that reinstalling fixes it — rather than asking you to sign in again, every time, with no explanation. • Removing a question bank now actually removes it. On in-house builds it was leaving the whole library behind on the phone, so the space was never freed and old questions could reappear if the bank was added back.

Installing on iPhone (sideloaded)

  1. Download the .ipa above.
  2. Open it with AltStore or Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Trust the developer profile: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  4. Open Master Prelims and sign in with your approved Google account.

Installing on Android

  1. Download the .apk above.
  2. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/files app when prompted.
  3. Open the APK to install, then sign in with your approved Google account.
