Installing on iPhone (sideloaded)

  1. Download the .ipa above.
  2. Open it with AltStore or Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Trust the developer profile: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  4. Open Master Prelims and sign in with your approved Google account.

Installing on Android

  1. Download the .apk above.
  2. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/files app when prompted.
  3. Open the APK to install, then sign in with your approved Google account.
